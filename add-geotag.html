How to Add GPS Coordinates to Your Photos Online

Geotagging is the process of attaching geographic metadata—specifically latitude, longitude, and altitude—directly into digital photos. Whether you captured a photo with a digital camera that lacked a built-in GPS receiver or need to update location data for business listings, GeotaggingPhotos makes it effortless to inject accurate coordinates in three simple steps:

1

Upload Your Image

Drag and drop your photo into the editor above. Our tool supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, and HEIC image formats up to 50MB per file.

2

Set & Adjust Location

Enter exact latitude and longitude values manually, click "Use Current Location", or drag the blue pin on the interactive map to place your geotag position.

3

Save & Download

Click "Save & Download Geotagged Photo". Your updated photo is generated instantly with embedded EXIF location metadata without altering image quality.

Why Location Metadata Matters for Modern Creators & Businesses

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) location data plays a critical role across multiple industries. By embedding verified GPS coordinates into your photography, you unlock distinct benefits:

travel_explore

Local SEO & Google Business Profiles

Local search engines and Google Maps prioritize images with authentic geographic tags. Geotagging store photos, service areas, and project sites signals location authority to local ranking algorithms.

real_estate_agent

Real Estate & MLS Property Listings

Real estate platforms such as Rightmove, Zoopla, and MLS syndications mandate precise location tagging to index residential and commercial properties accurately on interactive maps.

flight

Drone Pilots & Aerial Surveying

Mapping experts, orthomosaic creators, and UAV operators require strict EXIF latitude and longitude coordinates for GIS software compatibility and spatial site auditing.

photo_camera

Photographers & Travel Bloggers

Preserve the exact geographical origins of your shoots. Easily catalogue expedition captures across Lightroom, Apple Photos, and Google Photos with precise map coordinates.
