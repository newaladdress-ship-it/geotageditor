Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how GeotaggingPhotos.com uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. Introduction

GeotaggingPhotos.com ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our service and hold certain information. We believe in being transparent about how we collect and use data related to you.

By continuing to visit or use our services, you are agreeing to the use of cookies and similar technologies for the purposes we describe in this policy.

2. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, GeotaggingPhotos.com) are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies".

5. Our Privacy-First Approach

devices

100% Client-Side Processing

GeotaggingPhotos.com is built with a privacy-first architecture. When you upload photos to read or modify EXIF metadata (including location data), all processing happens directly within your web browser.

  • Your photos are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We do not use cookies to track the specific images you process or the geographic locations associated with them.
  • The data stays entirely on your local device.
