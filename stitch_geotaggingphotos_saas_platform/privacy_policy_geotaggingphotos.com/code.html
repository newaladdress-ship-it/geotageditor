waving_hand 1. Introduction

Welcome to GeotaggingPhotos.com. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our web application designed for viewing, editing, and managing photo EXIF metadata and GPS coordinates.

We built this tool with a strict privacy-by-design philosophy. As professionals handling sensitive geographical data, we understand that trust is paramount. This document outlines exactly what happens to your data when you drop a photo into our workspace.

database 2. Data Collection & Handling

2.1 Image Files & EXIF Metadata

When you use our tool, you are interacting with image files (JPEG, PNG, TIFF) that contain embedded metadata (Exchangeable Image File Format, or EXIF). This metadata often includes camera settings, dates, and precise GPS coordinates indicating where the photo was taken.

verified_user
Zero-Upload Guarantee

We do not upload, store, or transmit your image files or their associated EXIF data to our servers. All parsing and extraction happens entirely within your local browser environment.

2.2 Application Logs

Our web servers automatically record standard operational data necessary for security and diagnostics, including IP addresses, browser types, and access times. This data is transient and not linked to the content of the files you process.

memory 3. Client-Side Processing Architecture

GeotaggingPhotos.com utilizes modern Web APIs (such as the HTML5 File API and Web Workers) to process data.

  • Local Execution: When you drag and drop a photo into the processing zone, the browser reads the file directly from your local disk into memory.
  • No Server Transit: The metadata extraction scripts execute locally. Your network connection could drop completely, and the tool would continue to function.
  • Volatile Memory: Once you close the browser tab or hit "Clear Workspace," the parsed metadata and image blobs are purged from your browser's RAM immediately.

cookie 4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We maintain a minimal footprint to ensure a fast, frictionless experience.

Essential Functional Data

We may use `localStorage` within your browser to save interface preferences (e.g., dark/light mode toggle, map view state) so your workspace remains consistent between sessions. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics

We utilize privacy-respecting analytics (which do not set tracking cookies) solely to understand aggregate usage trends—such as the total number of sessions per day—to ensure server stability.

gavel 5. User Rights & Compliance

Because we do not collect or store personal data (as defined by GDPR or CCPA) beyond standard server access logs, typical requests for data portability or "right to be forgotten" regarding your photographs are structurally impossible—we simply do not have your data.

If you have questions regarding this architecture or our security practices, you may contact our engineering team directly at privacy@geotaggingphotos.com.
