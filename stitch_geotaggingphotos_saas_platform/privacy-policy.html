Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Client-Side Architecture

GeotaggingPhotos.com processes all image EXIF data locally inside your web browser. Your photo files, location data, and metadata are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect personal identification information unless voluntarily submitted via our contact form. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain web application preferences and anonymous telemetry analytics to monitor server uptime and page load speed.

4. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at Contact Support.
