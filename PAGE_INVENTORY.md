## Critical and high-priority issues

The live `/bulk-geotagging` route currently returns a blank viewport despite a populated source file. This is the only confirmed critical rendering blocker and must be fixed before any AdSense application. The Contact page's fake “message sent” behavior is a high trust issue because no form backend exists. The privacy/cookie disclosures are high priority because they describe generic analytics, advertising, cookies, and “no third-party sharing” behavior without distinguishing the actual Clarity script, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim requests, Google Fonts, CDN libraries, PWA localStorage, and the absence of a server-side contact endpoint.

The country pages are not automatically removed: they should remain only while they contain useful, differentiated information instead of country-name substitutions. No new country or city pages are planned. The legacy guide aliases must not be in the sitemap and should resolve to one canonical blog URL.

## Safe-fix scope

The implementation will repair the bulk page, improve tool error/loading/empty states, clarify privacy and format behavior, make the Contact form transparent, remove unsupported ranking claims and unverifiable social-proof language, improve schema/canonical/sitemap consistency, add accessible labels and navigation where low risk, and preserve all client-side photo processing. No advertising scripts or fake consent banners will be added.
