Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about photo geotagging, privacy, and metadata tools.

Are my photos uploaded to a remote server?

No! All EXIF metadata processing happens locally in your web browser using WebAssembly. Your original photos never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, and WebP image formats for geotagging and EXIF viewing.

How does geotagging help local SEO?

Injecting precise location coordinates into property and business photos signals geographical relevance to Google Image Search and local search algorithms.

Can I batch process multiple photos at once?

Yes! Use our Bulk Geotagging Tool to upload up to 500 images at a time and apply global coordinates instantly.
