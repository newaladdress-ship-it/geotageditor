Streamline Large Image Collections with Bulk Geotagging

Managing metadata for large photo libraries can be a daunting, repetitive task when editing files individually. GeoTaggingPhotos provides a high-throughput Bulk Geotagging Tool designed to inject verified latitude, longitude, and altitude coordinates into hundreds of photos simultaneously—eliminating manual overhead and saving hours of editing time.

1

Batch Upload

Select or drag up to 500 images at once into the queue. Supports high-resolution JPG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF file formats.

2

Set Global Coordinates

Enter your desired latitude and longitude preset. The global coordinates are mapped automatically across every image in your batch queue.

3

One-Click ZIP Export

Click "Process & Download ZIP". The browser creates a downloadable ZIP of processed copies. JPEG output is used when a source format needs conversion.

Who Needs Bulk Photo Geotagging?

High-volume metadata editing is essential across several fast-paced industries:

real_estate_agent Real Estate & Commercial Photography

Geotag entire property galleries in one action. Ensure interior and exterior images carry consistent GPS tags required for portal syndication and MLS mapping.

store Multi-Location Local SEO Agencies

Quickly tag batch photos for franchise store locations, client job sites, and local business service areas to support regional asset organization.

flight Drone Pilots & UAV Survey Teams

Assign spatial coordinates across aerial photo sets before importing into photogrammetry software like Pix4D, Agisoft Metashape, or QGIS.

construction Construction & Engineering Audits

Maintain verifiable geo-stamped photo documentation for site progress reports, contractor verification, and environmental compliance audits.

High-Speed Browser Processing

Our WebAssembly metadata engine processes your bulk uploads locally inside your browser. No files are stored on remote servers, providing total data privacy and zero upload bandwidth bottlenecking.
